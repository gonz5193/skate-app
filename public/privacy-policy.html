SKATE app. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8/30/2026

SKATE app ("we," "us," or "our") lets friends challenge each other to games of S-K-A-T-E by recording and posting video clips. This policy explains what information we collect when you use SKATE, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect the email address, username, and password you provide. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never stored by us in plain text.

When you use the app, we collect:

  • Video clips you record and upload
  • Game data: tricks called out, wins, losses, letters earned
  • Comments you post
  • Friend requests and friendships
  • Redo-vote flags and ballots you cast
  • Reports you file and users you block

2. How we use this information

We use this information to operate the app: to run games between you and your friends, display the public feed, show your stats and match history, and let you connect with friends. We do not sell your information to third parties.

3. Public visibility of your content

SKATE app's feed is public. Video clips, trick names, spots, usernames, comments, and game outcomes you post are visible to anyone using the app.

Technical note: video files are hosted at direct storage links. A clip's video file may remain technically reachable via its direct link even after it is removed from view within the app (for example, after a "redo" vote), until it is fully cleared during routine cleanup. Don't post anything in a clip you wouldn't want to be publicly viewable.

4. Third parties who process your data

We use the following service providers to run SKATE app. They process your data on our behalf and have their own privacy and security practices:

  • Supabase — our database, user authentication, and video storage provider
  • Vercel — our web hosting provider

We do not currently show ads or work with advertising partners. If that changes, we will update this policy before doing so and disclose what data, if any, is shared with ad networks.

5. Children's privacy

SKATE app is not intended for anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at the email below.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us (see Section 8). We will delete your account and personal data within a reasonable time, except where we're required to retain certain records by law.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can:

  • Update your username and password from within the app
  • Block other users, which hides their content from your feed
  • Report content that concerns you
  • Request full account deletion by emailing us

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including access controls on our database. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SKATE app grows. If we make material changes, we'll post the updated policy here and update the date at the top. Continued use of SKATE app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy, or want to request deletion of your account and data? Email us at skate.app.support@gmail.com.
